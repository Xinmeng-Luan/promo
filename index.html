PROMO: Physics-Informed Reduced-Order MOdal Neural Operator for Friction-Induced Vibration in Bowed Strings

Xinmeng Luan, Gary Scavone

This page presents 32 evaluation cases under different combinations of bow force $F_B$ and bow velocity $v_B$. For each case, both the ground truth and PROMO predicted audio and visual results are provided. The audio signals are synthesized from the displacement at the observation point $u(t,x_{BR})$. The accompanying figure shows the PROMO prediction (top), the ground truth (middle), and the point-wise absolute error (bottom).
